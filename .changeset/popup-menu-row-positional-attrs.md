Add `data-first`/`data-last` (first/last visible list-level row) and `data-first-in-group`/`data-last-in-group` (first/last visible row within a group) attributes to menu items, checkbox/radio items, submenu/subpage triggers, select/combobox items, groups, separators, empty, and loading parts. Attributes are filter-aware and disabled in virtualized mode.
